A good design would be a **segmented biodegradable multiblock copolymer** with:

- **Rigid, high-$T_g$ ester-rich hard segments** to raise thermal/mechanical stability.
- **Rubbery, biodegradable soft segments** to preserve toughness.
- **Controlled glycolide/carbonate/caprolactone content** to keep hydrolytic degradation in a clinically useful window.

## Proposed copolymer

\[
\textbf{[PLLA-co-PLGA]}-\textbf{b}-\textbf{[PTMC-co-PCL]}-\textbf{b}-\textbf{[PLLA-co-PLGA]}
\]

An ABA triblock or multiblock architecture:

### Hard segment: lactide/glycolide-rich block

\[
\text{poly(L-lactide-co-glycolide)}, \quad \text{LA:GA} \approx 85:15 \text{ to } 90:10
\]

Purpose:

- **L-lactide raises $T_g$** and modulus.
- A small amount of **glycolide increases hydrolytic degradability** and reduces excessive crystallinity.
- Keeping glycolide modest avoids very fast acidic degradation.

Typical hard-segment $T_g$:

\[
T_g \approx 50{-}60^\circ\text{C}
\]

### Soft segment: carbonate/caprolactone-rich block

\[
\text{poly(trimethylene carbonate-co-}\varepsilon\text{-caprolactone)}
\]

Suggested composition:

\[
\text{TMC:CL} \approx 70:30 \text{ to } 50:50
\]

Purpose:

- **PTMC provides elasticity and toughness**.
- **PCL improves ductility and tear resistance**.
- Both are biodegradable, but degrade more slowly than glycolide-rich PLGA, helping avoid overly rapid strength loss.

## Example target composition

A reasonable starting formulation would be:

\[
\boxed{
70\ \text{mol\% L-lactide}
:\ 10\ \text{mol\% glycolide}
:\ 15\ \text{mol\% trimethylene carbonate}
:\ 5\ \text{mol\% } \varepsilon\text{-caprolactone}
}
\]

with a segmented rather than fully random architecture.

## Why this design satisfies the goals

### 1. Increased $T_g$

The lactide-rich hard phase dominates the thermal response.

- PLLA has a relatively high $T_g$ near $55{-}65^\circ\text{C}$.
- Incorporating lactide-rich hard blocks increases the apparent $T_g$ compared with softer biodegradable polymers such as PCL or PTMC.

The copolymer should retain a clinically useful $T_g$ around or above body temperature, preferably:

\[
T_g \gtrsim 45^\circ\text{C}
\]

to prevent excessive softening at $37^\circ\text{C}$.

### 2. Preserved toughness

Pure PLLA is stiff but brittle. Toughness is improved by introducing rubbery biodegradable soft domains.

The PTMC/PCL block provides:

- segmental mobility,
- energy dissipation during deformation,
- improved elongation at break,
- reduced crack propagation.

A segmented architecture is important because it allows microphase separation:

- hard lactide-rich domains act as physical crosslinks,
- soft carbonate/caprolactone domains absorb deformation.

This gives better toughness than a purely random copolymer with the same average composition.

### 3. Clinically acceptable degradation rate

The degradation rate can be tuned by the lactide:glycolide:carbonate/caprolactone ratio.

- **Glycolide** accelerates hydrolysis.
- **Lactide** provides slower-degrading structural stability.
- **TMC and PCL** slow degradation and reduce brittle acidic autocatalysis.
- The absence of excessive glycolide avoids too-rapid mass loss.

A clinically reasonable target for many temporary implants would be:

- retention of useful mechanical strength for **6–12 weeks**,
- substantial degradation over **6–18 months**,
- degradation products compatible with normal metabolic clearance.

## Recommended architecture

Use a multiblock structure such as:

\[
\left[
\text{PLGA}_{85/15}
-
\text{P(TMC-co-CL)}
\right]_n
\]

or an ABA triblock:

\[
\text{PLGA}_{85/15}
-
\text{P(TMC-co-CL)}
-
\text{PLGA}_{85/15}
\]

with approximately:

- **65–80 wt% hard lactide/glycolide segment**
- **20–35 wt% soft TMC/caprolactone segment**

This range should increase $T_g$ while preserving ductility.

## Summary design

\[
\boxed{
\text{Segmented PLGA}_{85/15}\text{-}b\text{-P(TMC-co-}\varepsilon\text{CL)}
}
\]

with about **70 mol% lactide**, **10 mol% glycolide**, and **20 mol% soft carbonate/caprolactone comonomer**.

This design balances:

- higher $T_g$ from lactide-rich hard segments,
- toughness from PTMC/PCL soft domains,
- clinically acceptable biodegradation from controlled ester/carbonate hydrolysis.
